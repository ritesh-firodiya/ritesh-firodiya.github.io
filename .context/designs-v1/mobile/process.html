Process · idea → money

Eight stages, and every gate is a number.

How one person ships apps without a team. Not advice — the actual working document.

Most process writing stops at "it builds". That is halfway.

  1. 1 Idea

    One sentence: who, what, instead of what.

  2. 2 Research

    A keyword with real search volume exists.

  3. 3 Validate

    Incumbents are weak — measured from their negative reviews.

  4. 4 Build

    Designs approved, then schema approved. Two hard gates.

  5. 5 Launch

    Listing complete, review prompt actually shipping.

  6. 6 Market

    One channel, 30 days. A listing edit restarts re-indexing and invalidates the series.

  7. 7 Money

    Install → paid conversion is a known number.

  8. 8 Improve

    Ranked from review themes and funnel drop-off.

Stages 5–8 decide whether any of it was worth doing, and they are where a solo developer actually loses.
