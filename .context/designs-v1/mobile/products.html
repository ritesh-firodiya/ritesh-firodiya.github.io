Why this page exists

This site used to say something that was not true.

It promised no ads in the games and no subscriptions. One game has an ad banner and four of the apps are subscriptions.

The mistake was the shape of the claim: a promise for every app is only as true as the least convenient app. A fact per app cannot go stale that way.
