Tic Tac Toe

The classic 3×3, done properly — two players on one device, or a minimax AI that never loses on Hard.

Free · ad-supported No in-app purchases at all

This one is free because it shows you ads.

A banner sits at the bottom of the board, and a full-screen ad appears when a game ends. There is nothing to buy — no ad-free tier, no unlock, no subscription. If that trade is not one you want, no other app of mine has ads at all.

Banner
Bottom of the board, always on screen
Interstitial
Full screen, when a game finishes
Rewarded
None — not used

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which means the app needs a network connection and AdMob receives an advertising identifier from your device. On iOS you are asked before that happens, and saying no does not change the game.
