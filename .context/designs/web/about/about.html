Nine years of production TypeScript across web, mobile and backend — Walmart, Swiggy, Globant, Speechify. Comfortable from a Postgres schema to a Play Store release, and usually the only person who touches both.

For the last while I have been building my own products for India — thirteen of them, four you can use today — plus agent tooling on the Anthropic SDK and MCP.

Building alone is what taught me to draw every screen before writing any of it. A component that appears in no HTML file does not ship, and that rule has cost me far less than the rewrites it prevented.

Pune, India. Open to senior and staff IC roles, tech-lead roles, and to consulting.
