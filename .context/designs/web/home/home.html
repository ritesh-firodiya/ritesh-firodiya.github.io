zeno

Rules, knowledge and a decision gate for teams that build with Claude Code.

  • Weighs a request before acting. DO IT, DO IT DIFFERENTLY or PUSH BACK, each reason cited.
  • Finds what was decided. Every wiki and decision record is searchable by Claude.
  • Turns hard rules into checks. Layout, wiki, copies and each repo's architecture.
