Aakalan · privacy policy

What Aakalan stores, and what it does not.

Last updated 20 September 2026

What is not collected

No advertising identifier. No location. No contacts — adding someone to a group uses a name you type, not your address book. No analytics SDK is linked into the app.

This section is first because it is the one people open the page to check.

What is collected

The expenses, groups and people you enter, and an email address if you create an account. Nothing else, and nothing in the background.

Crash reports

A crash sends a stack trace, the device model and the app version. A report can contain the name of the screen you were on. It does not contain your expenses.

Deleting everything

Settings → Account → Delete account removes it all within thirty days, backups included. You can also email a deletion request and it will be handled the same way.
