Aakalan · Privacy policy

What Aakalan stores, and what it does not.

Last updated 20 September 2026

What is collected

The expenses, groups and people you enter. An email address, if you create an account. Nothing else is collected, and nothing is collected in the background.

What is not collected

No advertising identifiers. No location. No contacts — adding someone to a group uses a name you type, not your address book. No analytics SDK is linked into the app.

Crash reporting

Crashes are reported with a stack trace, the device model and the app version. A crash report can contain a screen name. It does not contain your expenses.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account removes everything within thirty days, including backups. You can also email a deletion request.
