How I build

From an idea to a product people use, in eight stages.

Every project goes through the same stages in the same order. Each one leaves something behind that you can open.

  1. 01

    Idea

    A problem I have, or one I keep seeing other people have.

    Leaves behindA one-paragraph thesis

  2. 02

    Research

    Who else solves it, what they charge, and where they fail.

    Leaves behindSource notes and a research brief

  3. 03

    Define

    What is in version one, what is not, and the names and numbers everything else must match.

    Leaves behindDecisions and a feature list

  4. 04

    Design

    Every screen and every state, as a clickable HTML page.

    Leaves behindA design set and a flow chart

  5. 05

    Build

    The data model first, then the app against the designs.

    Leaves behindThe app, with tests

  6. 06

    Release

    Store listing, screenshots, privacy, review.

    Leaves behindA build people can install

  7. 07

    Market

    Positioning, store search, and somewhere to say it.

    Leaves behindA listing people can find

  8. 08

    Operate

    Numbers, support, and the fixes they point to.

    Leaves behindMetrics and release notes
