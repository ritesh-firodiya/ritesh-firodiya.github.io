How do I stop a subscription renewing?

In your Google Play or App Store account, not in the app — neither store lets a developer cancel on your behalf. Access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for. AskCal, Aakalan, Plan-Kid and Scrvio are the subscriptions.

Which apps have ads?

One: Tic Tac Toe, with a banner and an interstitial between games. There is no ad-free purchase for it. Nothing else has an ad SDK at all.

I bought something and got a new phone.

Open the app, go to the purchase screen and tap Restore — purchases are tied to your store account, not the device. If it still does not appear, email the store receipt.

Do you track me?

Only Chitragupt carries analytics, and it asks first. Tic Tac Toe's ad network receives an advertising identifier. Every other app has no analytics SDK at all.
