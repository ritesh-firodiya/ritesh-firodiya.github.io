AI forgets your domain

Why a price is what it is gets said once, then lost. Every session starts fresh. Last month's "no" returns as a new idea. It sounds sure, builds it, and you find out later.

How canon-cli fixes it

    A wiki of decisions, prices, rules and features in each repo. Searched locally, before the AI answers. A gate weighs each request: do it, do it differently, or push back. Every claim is marked backed or not, and every reason names its source. Checks fail when a rule breaks. Overrides are logged, and canon proposes better rules from them.
