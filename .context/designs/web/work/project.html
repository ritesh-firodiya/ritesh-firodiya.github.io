The problem

Every Indian tax calculator answers one question: which regime is cheaper for you, this year. That is the wrong unit. Tax in a household moves across people — a parent's medical premium, a spouse's home-loan interest, a child's tuition — and filed one at a time, a family leaves a deduction on the table.

What it does

  • Four pillars, one ledger. Inbox keeps the documents; Tax, Expense and Portfolio turn them into answers.
  • Parses what you upload. Form 16, AIS, 26AS and bank statements go in as files, not retyped numbers.
  • Both regimes, side by side. With the right ITR form picked and the delta in rupees.
  • CA-ready handoff. A one-click PDF, or a verified CA if you would rather hand it over.

Decisions worth defending

  • Zod as the only contract. Tax rules change every budget. One schema for the form, the function and the stored document makes a rule change one edit.
  • 189 small functions, not one service. Each deduction is its own function with its own tests, so a wrong number traces to one file.
  • The verdict is free. Which regime wins, and by how much, costs nothing. The paid tiers are the workings and the household lens.
