Your domain knowledge, kept local. Enforced.

zeno is a productivity tool for the whole product lifecycle. Your rules, prices and past decisions stay in your repo, and the AI checks them before it acts. It guesses less, and you ship faster.

AI forgets your domain

Knowledge lives in chat

Why a price is what it is gets said once, then lost.

Settled things come back

Every session starts fresh. Last month's "no" returns as a new idea.

The AI fills gaps by guessing

It sounds sure, builds it, and you find out later.

How zeno fixes it

  1. 1

    Know

    A wiki of decisions, prices, rules and features in each repo. Searched locally, before the AI answers.

  2. 2

    Enforce

    A gate weighs each request: do it, do it differently, or push back. Every reason names its source.

  3. 3

    Ship

    Checks fail when a rule breaks. Overrides are logged, and zeno proposes better rules from them.
