## Why it is drawn this way
**The full list lives here, not on Home.** Home carries four rows; eight would
turn the landing page into a CV.

**Groups, not a tag cloud.** A wall of forty logos says nothing about depth. Six
named groups with the real tools survives a reader who knows the domain.

**The third paragraph is the one that matters.** Building alone is what forced
the draw-before-you-build habit, and that is the thing a client is actually
buying.

## The voice, which is the same on every screen
Specific over general — a number where there is one. Headings make a claim or
state a fact, never name a category. Labels are nouns; buttons say what happens
next. The thing first, then the caveat. No unlock, no empower, no seamless.
Every gap is stated with its reason rather than left to be noticed.
