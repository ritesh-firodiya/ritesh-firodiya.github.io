## Why it is drawn this way

**Experience lives here, not on Home.** Home carries four rows; eight would turn
the landing page into a CV. The full list is one click away for the reader who
wants it and invisible to the one who does not.

**The skills grid is groups, not a tag cloud.** A wall of forty logos says
nothing about depth. Six named groups with the real tools in each is scannable
and survives a reader who knows the domain.
