## Why it is drawn this way

**A 14rem label column against a 62ch measure.** Long prose at full page width is
unreadable, and a centred column wastes the left edge that the labels make useful.

**The honest failure is the body of the piece.** The interesting part of
Chitragupt is not its stack — it is that an earlier version of this very site
called it free while its config defined three priced tiers. A case study that
only reports success is a brochure.
