## Why it is drawn this way
**A label column against a 62ch measure.** Long prose at full width is unreadable
and a centred column wastes the left edge the labels make useful.

**The honest failure is the body of the piece.** The interesting part is not the
stack — it is that an earlier version of this site called the product free while
its config defined three priced tiers. A case study that only reports success is
a brochure.

**The counts band repeats Home's device** so the two pages read as one system,
and every number in it is checkable.

## The voice, which is the same on every screen
Specific over general — a number where there is one. Headings make a claim or
state a fact, never name a category. Labels are nouns; buttons say what happens
next. The thing first, then the caveat. No unlock, no empower, no seamless.
Every gap is stated with its reason rather than left to be noticed.
