## Why it is drawn this way

**Email, not a form.** The site is a static export with no server to post to. A
contact form that silently drops messages is worse than no form, and a
third-party form handler would put a stranger between a job offer and an inbox.

**Four tiles, each naming who it is for.** Email for anyone, LinkedIn for
recruiters, GitHub with the caveat that most product repos are private, and the
timezone — which is the question every remote conversation opens with.
