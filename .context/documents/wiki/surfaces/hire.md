## Why it is drawn this way
**No rate card.** The number depends on scope; publishing one produces a figure
wrong for both sides and opens the conversation with a correction.

**Time is in the right-hand column of every engagement**, because it is the
question that actually gates the decision.

**"What I will say no to" is on the page.** It disqualifies bad-fit work before a
call instead of during one, and a list of refusals is the most credible thing on
any services page.

## The voice, which is the same on every screen
Specific over general — a number where there is one. Headings make a claim or
state a fact, never name a category. Labels are nouns; buttons say what happens
next. The thing first, then the caveat. No unlock, no empower, no seamless.
Every gap is stated with its reason rather than left to be noticed.
