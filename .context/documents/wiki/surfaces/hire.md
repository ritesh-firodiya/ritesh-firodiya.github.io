## Why it is drawn this way

**No rate card.** The number depends on scope. Publishing one produces a figure
that is wrong for both sides and starts the conversation with a correction.

**"What I will say no to" is on the page.** It disqualifies badly-fitting work
before a call rather than during one, and a list of refusals is the most credible
thing on a services page.

**The process names the approval gate.** Designs first, and the client approves
them before anything is built — the same rule this set is written under.
