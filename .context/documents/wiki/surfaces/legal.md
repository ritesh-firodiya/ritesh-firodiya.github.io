## Why it is drawn this way
**These URLs cannot move.** Two live Play listings name them and six shipped
builds hardcode them. Installed apps never update, and a 404 here is grounds for
an app to be pulled. The heading says exactly that rather than "Legal".

**A table, not a nav list.** Someone arrives from a store listing wanting one
document for one app; product down the side and document across the top answers
that in a glance.

**"Not applicable" carries a reason on hover** — an app with no account has
nothing to delete, and a blank cell would read as an omission.

## The voice, which is the same on every screen
Specific over general — a number where there is one. Headings make a claim or
state a fact, never name a category. Labels are nouns; buttons say what happens
next. The thing first, then the caveat. No unlock, no empower, no seamless.
Every gap is stated with its reason rather than left to be noticed.
