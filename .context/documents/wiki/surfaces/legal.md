## Summary
Every policy for every product, at stable URLs.

## Raw wireframe

## Why it is drawn this way

**These URLs cannot move.** Two live Play listings use them as their
privacy-policy URL and six shipped builds hardcode them. Installed builds never
update, and a 404 here is grounds for an app to be pulled.

**A table, not a nav list.** Someone arrives from a store listing looking for one
document for one app. Product down the side and document across the top answers
that in one glance.
