## Why it is drawn this way

**One column at 68ch and nothing else.** No cards, no pull quotes, no sidebar. The
reader has a specific question and leaves once it is answered.

**"What is not collected" is its own heading.** It is the thing people actually
came to check, and burying it inside a paragraph about collection makes them read
the whole page to find out.
