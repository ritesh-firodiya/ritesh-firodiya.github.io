## Why it is drawn this way

**The model sits above the install button.** Never below it, never in a footnote,
never softened into "unlock". This is the rule the site exists to enforce.

**An unavailable platform renders disabled with its reason**, never hidden and
never linked to a track most people cannot open. Web is struck through here with
"no web client — the ledger is phone-only" beside it.

**Drawn subscribed, shipping on two stores, with analytics to disclose.** The
easy case hides every layout problem the hard one has.
