## Summary
The printable record, and the PDF.

## Raw wireframe

## Why it is drawn this way

**One column, no cards.** A résumé is read linearly and is often printed. Cards,
sidebars and pull quotes all break that.

**The PDF is a peer of the page, not a replacement.** Recruiters ask for a file;
everyone else would rather not download one. Both exist and neither is hidden
behind the other.
