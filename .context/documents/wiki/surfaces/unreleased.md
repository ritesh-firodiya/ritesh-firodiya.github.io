## Summary
A product that exists as a spec and nothing else.

## Raw wireframe
- .context/designs/web/products/unreleased.html

## Why it is drawn this way

**Not a state of Product.** "Unreleased" is not in the closed set
(empty · loading · error · offline · locked), and §5 says anything the closed set
cannot name is its own screen. It is genuinely a different page.

**There is no disabled install button.** A greyed-out button invites a click that
can never work. The space it would take carries the reason instead.

**No waitlist.** A waitlist for a product with no delivery date collects
addresses and disappoints them later.
