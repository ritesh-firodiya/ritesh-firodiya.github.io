What actually changed

  • — The information architecture: new routes, work promoted, the orphaned case study given an index.
  • — A dark palette, as a token remap.

What did not change, and should have

  • — Same warm-paper ground, same near-black ink, same burnt-sienna accent.
  • — Same Fraunces display serif over Inter.
  • — Same language of hairline-bordered cards in a centred container, same pill buttons, same mono uppercase labels.

Structure moved; the surface did not. That reads as the same site rearranged — which is what you saw.

What all three keep

Showcase the work · showcase the experience · be the brand and legal site for every product · hire me · get in touch · work together.

The model above every install button. No claim on behalf of every app. An unavailable platform disabled with its reason. A missing asset visibly missing.

The three

Click a title to open it full size · previews are live

A · Index →

White · Inter Tight 900 · vermilion

The page is a list. No cards, no boxes, no shadows — a rule, a number, a name. Work and products are one index because a reader cares what a thing is, not which bucket it sits in. Hovering a row inverts it to black and floats the real screenshot at your cursor.

Strongest
— confident, gallery-like, unmistakably not a template. Scales to 40 products without redesign.
Weakest
— austere. Almost no colour, and the work has to carry it.
B · System →

Near-black · JetBrains Mono · amber

The premise becomes the interface. A status bar carrying live counts, numbered sections, a visible column grid, every product a checked row with per-platform state, and nine years drawn as bars on a timeline rather than listed. The “every fact is verified” claim stops being a sentence and becomes the layout.

Strongest
— peers and technical readers. Most distinctive, and most true to what you actually do.
Weakest
— dense, and dark mono portfolios are a crowded genre. Least warm of the three.
C · Poster →

Cream · Space Grotesk · teal + coral

Colour-blocked and asymmetric. Full-bleed coral and teal fields, hard offset shadows instead of soft cards, oversized numerals, slabs tilted a degree off axis. The most designed-looking of the three and the one a visitor is most likely to remember.

Strongest
— memorable, warm, reads as a studio with a point of view. Best for clients.
Weakest
— the loudest. A dense legal or product table has to sit inside it without fighting it.
