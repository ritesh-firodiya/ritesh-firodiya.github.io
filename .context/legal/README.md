# Legal — the portfolio

This site hosts every app's privacy policy and terms: `public/legal/<app>/`
plus `public/legal/terms.html`, served at https://ritesh-firodiya.github.io/legal/.
A store listing links there, so the copy in `public/legal/` is the one users read.

Some apps also keep a `privacy-policy.html` in their own `.context/legal/`.
Nothing syncs the two, and they differ (charades, 2026-10-27).

The site itself collects nothing: no analytics script, no cookies, no forms that post.
