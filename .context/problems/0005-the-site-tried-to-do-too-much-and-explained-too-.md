# The site tried to do too much and explained too much

## Summary
The site kept growing pages and words. One version had nine text pages with no pictures, and eight of twelve routes held no image. A later design gave it six goals and a route or band for each.

## Impact
A reader had to work to reach the work. The first product sat at the fourth band of the home page.

## Expected
The reader sees the work first, and no page explains itself. The current choice and what was rejected are in [[site]]. Removed pages forward, see [[routes]].
