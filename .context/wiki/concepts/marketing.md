# Marketing and measuring

1. **One context file per product.** Each product's `marketing/context.md` holds identity, value, audience, price model, status and markets; charades' is the model. This page is the craft.
2. **Position on what the category gets wrong** (no ads or subscription; one payment against weekly plans; asking instead of guessing).
3. **Competitor reviews are the brief**: group rivals' negative reviews by complaint.
4. **Zero ratings is the first problem.** Fix it in the app (a review prompt after the third finished round) before other marketing.
5. **Skip the head term; find the weak niche** whose leaders rate lowest.
6. **After a listing edit, change nothing and scan again**; another edit restarts re-indexing.
7. **A global app names no country.** A second locale goes on both stores, or the reason goes in `tasks.md`.
8. **Publish the honest number**; claim no accuracy figure you have not measured.
9. **No dates around a blocked step.**
10. **Press kit: source art lives in `designs/assets/`**; `marketing/press/media/` holds exports only.
11. **Contact address**: every product uses the founder's Gmail address (each `marketing/context.md` names it) until its domain has mail records.

## Keeping numbers honest
12. **A failed scrape is not "not ranked"**: record null, key every artefact by date, name what a total leaves out.
13. **Define a metric before its first number** in `metrics/definitions.yml` (Play counts devices; many installs never open the app).
