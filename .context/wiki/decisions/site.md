# The portfolio site

**Choice:**
- One reader: a hiring manager for a senior, staff or tech-lead role. App users and store reviewers get Support and Legal from the footer.
- Look: LAUNCH, dark first (near-black, violet brand, cyan accent, glass cards); light is a remap. Every layout is written phone-first.
- Home is three parts: who → canon (Know → Enforce → Ship, then what it caught, about the domain, not code) → Work (three large cards in the order AskCal, Aakalan, Charades, then small tiles). Project pages share one template.
- No page explains itself: no intro paragraphs, closing bands or facts rows. Counts are derived, never typed.
- Every project's wiki is published in full; the build stops on a credential-shaped string. Pictures are read from each product's `.context/`, never copied here.
- Chitragupt is shown as "Pre-launch".

**Why:** the owner's reviews: earlier versions served six goals, over-explained, "looks plain", "wrong story", "too long / dense".

**Rejected:** separate Work, Process, About, Contact and Hire pages · a per-project process dot board · a stats row · the PROOF and INDEX looks · an allow-list for published wiki pages · prices on the site.
