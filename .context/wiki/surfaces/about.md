## Why it is drawn this way
**The facts sit beside the story, not under it.** Location, years and what I am open to are in a card next to the portrait, so a reader who only wants those never has to read three paragraphs.

**"How I work" is three habits, each one checkable on this site.** Draw it first links to the design sets, write down why links to the wikis. A value nobody can verify is decoration.

**Skills are grouped, at the end.** They are a lookup table for a recruiter matching keywords, so they are complete and they are last.
