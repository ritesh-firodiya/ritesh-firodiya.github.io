## Why it is drawn this way
**Email is the page.** It is the largest thing on it, and it is a mailto, not a form. A static site has nowhere to post a form to, and a form that goes to a third party is a dependency for no gain.

**What I am open to is stated, in order.** Full-time roles first, short contracts second. The old site had a separate Hire page for consulting; it competed with the résumé and is folded into three lines here. `/hire` redirects to this page.

**App users get their own door.** Someone with a purchase problem should not write to the hiring inbox with no context, so Support is linked from the bottom.
