## Why it is drawn this way
**Redrawn 2026-10-23 (LAUNCH)** — see [[2026-10-23-launch-redesign]]. Three parts: who → zeno (Know → Enforce → Ship, then three domain verdicts it stopped) → Work (three large cards, twelve small tiles). Dark first, glow and gradient headline. The process board is gone; the stages are on [[zeno]]. The paragraphs below describe the 2026-10-04 layout and are kept for history.

**One page, three parts.** Who I am is one sentence and three buttons. Work is every project. Process is one board. Work, Process, About and Contact used to be four more pages that repeated what was here; their addresses forward (2026-10-04, review round 4).

**Every project is one card, all the same size** (2026-10-12). Four to a row from tablet up, two on a phone, in the order of `products.json`: the leads, then the phone apps, then the web products, then the rest. The picture fills the card (11:9), and a short see-through, blurred panel lies over its foot: name, state, tags and counts. The tagline is on the project page. The owner chose the see-through panel on 2026-10-12; its known cost is weaker text contrast over a dark picture, which is why the counts use `ink-2` here. A store screenshot with no device and no backdrop (Chess) is drawn as a phone on the product's brand colour, at the size the other apps' store art shows one. The panel rounds its own bottom corners, because a backdrop blur is not clipped by the card's. A project with no picture keeps its card and shows a hatched slot.

**A card says what the project is before it is opened:** the release state, a Mobile or Web tag, and how many screens and wiki pages it left behind. A product with a site and an app carries both tags and shows a phone standing beside its web screen.

**The pictures are each product's own.** Store art from its listing, or one of its design screens embedded live. Nothing is copied into this repo.

**A project with nothing to show is a row**, not a card with an empty picture.

**The board is the process.** The eight stages head the columns, each with what it leaves behind, and every project is a row of dots counted from its own repository. On a phone it is a key and a list, not a table that scrolls sideways.

**Nothing is said twice.** No stats row, no closing band. The email is in the header and the footer, on every page.
