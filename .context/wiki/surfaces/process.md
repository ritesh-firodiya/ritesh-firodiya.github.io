## Summary
The eight stages every product goes through, where each project is on them, and the design set and wiki each one left behind.

## Why it is drawn this way
**Eight stages, not one habit.** An earlier draft opened with "drawn, written down, then built". The owner's correction (2026-10-04): the process is the whole life of a product — finding the idea, market research, definition, design, development, release, marketing, running it. Design is stage four.

**Each stage names what it leaves behind.** A stage with no artefact is a slogan. The artefact is what a reader can go and open.

**The board is the page.** Projects down, stages across, one dot per cell. It answers "how far has this person actually taken things" for eight projects at once, and it shows the gaps: most projects have no marketing and almost none are measured.

**A cell is counted, not claimed.** Done means the folder that stage writes to has content in that project's repo; under way means some. The build reads it from the repos; nothing here is typed by hand.

**The design sets and wikis are listed under the board**, with a link to each flow chart and each wiki. A withheld set is listed with its reason.

**Everything in every wiki is public** (decided 2026-10-04). The build scans each page for credential shapes and stops on a hit.
