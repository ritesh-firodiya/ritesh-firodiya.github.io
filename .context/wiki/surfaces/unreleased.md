## Why it is drawn this way
**It is the same page.** A project in design or in build has no row of buttons, and the rest is unchanged. An earlier draft drew disabled buttons with their reasons; that was three lines explaining an absence the state pill already states.

**The picture is a design screen, live.** There is no store art yet, so the product names one of its own screens. With a site and an app, it names one of each.

**The screens and the wiki are the content.** For a project this early they are everything there is, so they are the first links under the paragraph.
