## Why it is drawn this way
**One index, not two.** The old site had Work (one case study) and Products (a table of how each app is paid for). This is the only list, and `/products/` redirects to it.

**The order is the owner's, not the alphabet's.** AskCal, Aakalan, Charades, then the rest (2026-10-04).

**The state pill is the first thing on a card.** Green is installable today, amber is a build behind a closed door, violet is code with nothing to install, slate is designs only.

**Every picture is the product's own.** Store art comes from the product's listing folder. Chitragupt has no web screenshot, so its card embeds its own design screen, live — which is also why it follows the light and dark toggle. Nothing is copied into this repo.

**Cards for what has a build, rows for what does not.** Six projects have nothing to show yet. As cards they were six hatched boxes filling half the page; as rows they take a fifth of it and still say what each one is.

**The filter is by state.** There is no empty state: every filter has at least two projects.
