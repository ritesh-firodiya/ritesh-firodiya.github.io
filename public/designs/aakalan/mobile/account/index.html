Account

Purpose: Everything that isn't money, on one scrollable screen.

It is a pushed screen now, not a tab. It is reached by tapping your own avatar — which is how it was always reached anyway. A screen visited twice a year does not earn one of the five most valuable slots in the app; the tab it gave up went to Spending, which people open every week.

One screen, not four. Profile is three fields, export is one button, privacy is a paragraph. None of them earns a destination of its own.

Your UPI ID appears once. It used to be printed in the profile card and in the row below it, on the same screen — the same string twice, six rows apart, one of them not tappable. The card identifies you; the row is where you change it.

One "How people pay you" screen, not two. Your UPI IDs and your phone numbers were heading for a screen each, and they are one topic: how somebody reaches you and how they send you money. Both are lists — one person routinely has a personal number and a work one, a personal VPA and a work VPA — and which to use is the payee's call, so all of them are kept and all of them are offered.

Export is a first-class row, not a footnote — product rule 4: exporting your own data is never Pro. It sits directly above the Pro card, deliberately, so it is obvious that the paywall does not touch it.

Two notification settings, and neither is fake. There used to be three, one of which was drawn as a switch and then labelled "can't be turned off". A control that cannot be operated is not a control — that one is a sentence now. The third, a weekly nudge about old debts, is gone: an app that messages your friends on a timer is doing something you did not ask for in a relationship you have to maintain.

Delete account is present, real, and states what happens to shared groups. Required by both stores, and the honest answer — your expenses stay in groups so other people's balances don't break — is better said here than discovered later.

What Aakalan does not do

· Never uploads your address book — matching happens on your device, one scrambled number at a time.

· Never sees a UPI PIN, card or bank credential. Payments hand off to your UPI app.

· Never sells or shares your expense data. Your "Just me" expenses are visible to you alone.

Adding someone to a group does share their name and number with the others in it — that is how they see whose row is whose.
