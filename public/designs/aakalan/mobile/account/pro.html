Aakalan Pro

Purpose: The only paywall in the app, and one screen — a pricing table and a plan picker are the same screen.

It leads with what stays free. That is not generosity, it is conversion: an Indian user who suspects the free tier is a trap closes the app, and Splitwise capping free expenses is the reason this one exists. The free list is longer than the Pro list, on purpose, and it is above it.

Nothing here gates adding, splitting, settling, or exporting — product rule 4. Pro is depth (history, currencies, recurring), never the core loop.

Annual is pre-selected with the monthly equivalent printed — ₹749/yr shown as ₹62/mo — so the comparison needs no arithmetic. Product rule 3 applies to pricing too.

Restore purchases is visible without scrolling. App Store review rejects without it, and a user who paid on another device and can't find it will ask for a refund rather than hunt.

Auto-renew terms are on-screen, not behind a link. Also an App Store requirement, and cheaper to satisfy than to appeal.

Wiring: the two plan cards map to RevenueCat's monthly and annual packages on the default offering; prices render from package.product.priceString, never hardcoded, so a regional price change doesn't ship a lie.
