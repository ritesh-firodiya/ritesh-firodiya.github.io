Recurring expenses

Purpose: Rent, wifi, the maid. The bill that is identical every month is the one people forget to log, and the one that most distorts a month when it is missing.

A rule, not a queue. The rule holds the title, amount, category, who it is with and how it splits. It produces a real expense on its due date and nothing at all before then. No greyed-out "upcoming" rows appear in the ledger, because an expense that has not happened is not an expense and must not move a balance.

It runs when the app opens, and it catches up honestly. Away for two months means two expenses appear, each dated to its own due date — never one merged bill for the period. A phone that never opens the app is the normal case, so "runs on the 1st" has to mean "is dated the 1st", not "requires you to be there on the 1st".

Never silent. Product rule 5 does not bend for automation: each generated expense gets an activity entry saying which rule made it, and the first screen after a catch-up carries a line naming what was added, with Undo. Money appearing in a shared ledger with no visible cause is how a splitter loses trust.

Pause rather than delete. A flat you leave for three months is paused; a flat you leave for good is deleted. Deleting a rule never touches the expenses it already made — those happened.

If the group changes underneath it, the rule stops and asks. A member leaving mid-tenancy means the split the rule stored is no longer a split anyone agreed to, and guessing a new one would silently move real money.

Pro. Depth, not the core loop: the same expense can always be added by hand, free, in three taps. That is the test every Pro gate in this app has to pass.
