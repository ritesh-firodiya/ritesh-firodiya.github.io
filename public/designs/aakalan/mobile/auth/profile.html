Your profile

Purpose: The last screen before the app. It collects exactly two things and then gets out of the way.

Nothing else is asked here. Contacts are requested when someone adds a member. A first group is created when someone wants one. Neither belongs in signing up.

Name is required, UPI ID is not — and the difference is stated. A UPI ID is what lets friends pay you in one tap, which is the app's whole edge, so it is asked for here with the reason attached rather than buried in settings. But blocking entry on it would cost more users than it gains.

The avatar colour is derived from the name, not chosen. One less decision, and it stays stable everywhere the person appears.
