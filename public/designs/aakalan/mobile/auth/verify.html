Purpose: Get past the SMS as fast as the OS allows.

Auto-fills from the SMS via one-time-code autofill on both platforms, and submits itself on the sixth digit. On a good day this screen is visible for two seconds and never touched.

The resend timer starts immediately and the number is editable from here — the two things people need when the SMS doesn't arrive, both on screen instead of behind a back button.

Errors are inline and specific. "Wrong code" and "This code expired" are different problems with different fixes, so they get different messages.
