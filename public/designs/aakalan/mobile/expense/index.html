Expense detail

Purpose: The full breakdown of one bill, and the place edits and deletions happen.

Your share is the headline, the bill total is the subtitle. The total is context; what you owe or lent is the fact. Leading with the ₹18,000 and hiding the ₹3,600 in small grey text would be backwards for the person reading it.

Every participant row shows paid vs owes vs net on one line, so the arithmetic is done for you. Product rule 3.

The history section is append-only and cannot be cleared. Product rule 5: an expense can be removed, the record that someone removed it cannot. Deleting from here does not erase this screen — it adds a final entry to it.

Frame 2 is the personal-expense version. Same screen, minus everything about other people. A solo expense is not a degraded group expense; it just has less to say.

There is no note and no comment thread. "What was this ₹6,200 for" is the question that starts the argument, and this screen answers it three times already: the description names the expense, the category groups it, and the receipt photo is the bill. A free-text box is a fourth answer to a question that has three — and the version with replies was a messaging product growing inside an app whose people list deliberately has no social graph, competing with the WhatsApp group these five are already in.

The thing that could not be said elsewhere is said in the History. If someone changes an amount, the log records what changed and by how much. That is the entry people actually go looking for, and it cannot be edited or cleared.
