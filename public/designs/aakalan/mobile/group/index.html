Group detail

Purpose: Answer three questions in the order people actually ask them — what's my position here, with whom, and what happened.

Simplify debts is one control, in Settings — it used to be two. There was a toggle here and a "Simplify debts by default" switch on the group settings screen, both governing the same list. Two controls over one behaviour is two places to look when the numbers change shape, and nobody could say which one won.

It belongs in Settings because it is a group decision, not a view. Simplification rewrites who pays whom for everybody, so five people flipping it back and forth on the main screen is five people seeing five different instructions for the same debts. Agreeing it once, where the rest of the group's rules live, is the honest shape.

The second frame is the same group with it on: five pairwise debts collapse into three transfers. The ledger is unchanged — simplification only rewrites who pays whom, never what anyone owes in total. That distinction is stated on screen because getting it wrong destroys trust in the numbers.

Every expense row carries your share, not just the bill total. "Dinner ₹6,200" tells you nothing; "₹6,200, your share ₹1,240, you lent ₹4,960" tells you everything.

Add is scoped here. The FAB on a group screen pre-selects that group, so the common case — add another Goa expense while looking at Goa — is zero extra taps.
