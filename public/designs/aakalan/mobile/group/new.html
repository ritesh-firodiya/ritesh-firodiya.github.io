New group

Purpose: Get from "I want to split this trip" to a usable group in one screen.

Adding people is creating the group, so it happens here rather than in a second step. The invite link is a share sheet, not a destination.

Only the name is required. Type "Goa Trip", press Create, and you have a working group — you can add people from inside it. Forcing member selection before the group exists is the reason group-creation funnels leak.

There is no "Type" picker. Trip / Home / Food / Other set the cover colour and the default category, and its own help text admitted it did "nothing else". That is a decision asked of someone who came here to split a trip, in exchange for a colour. The cover is now derived from the name, exactly as avatar colours already are — the design praised that as "one less decision, and it stays stable everywhere", and there was no reason groups were exempt.

Contacts permission is primed here, at the moment it is actually needed, with the reason on the button — never on day one, before the user has met the feature.

Frame 2 shows the state after tapping "Invite by link", with the group already created behind it. A person who joins by link sees the group before signing up.

The share targets are not drawn. This frame used to render a grid of WhatsApp / SMS / QR / More tiles in our own card component — a picture of the OS share sheet made out of parts that are not the OS share sheet. We do not choose those targets, cannot style them, and they differ per phone and per install, so drawing them promised a screen the user will never see. What is shown is the two things we actually control: the link, and the button that hands off. It also removed a WhatsApp green from an app that reserves green for money direction.
