Group settings

Purpose: Everything about the group that isn't an expense. Reached from the gear on group detail, and rarely visited by design.

The invite link lives here, as a row, and as a share sheet from group creation. A link and four share targets do not need a screen of their own.

Leaving with an open balance is blocked, and says why. Silently allowing it makes a debt vanish from one side and persist on the other — the single worst bug this class of app can have. The button is disabled with the reason on it, not hidden.

Currency is per group, because a trip abroad and a flat in Bangalore are different currencies for the same person. Free is INR-only; changing it is the multi-currency Pro feature, and it is gated before any expense is entered so nobody loses work to a paywall.

Deleting a group is destructive and permanent, so it is last, rose, and asks. Everything above it is reversible.
