Settle up · one screen, two directions

Purpose: The payoff loop, and the thing Splitwise cannot do in India — pay over UPI without leaving the app.

Confirmation is for the side that cannot know. It used to run both ways, and one of those ways was nonsense. When the payer says "I paid you", that claim reduces what the payer owes and only the receiver can check it — so it waits. When the receiver says "they paid me", it reduces what somebody else owes, the person recording it is the only one who could possibly know, and there is nobody to protect. Asking the debtor to confirm that they paid you is asking permission to forgive a debt.

So: you recording money you received is final and immediate. The balance moves, the row clears, no waiting state, no "Unconfirmed" flag, no second screen. You recording money you sent still waits — that half of product rule 7 is the half that was ever doing any work.

One screen, and it is reached with the person already chosen. Every balance row carries its own settle button. The separate "Settle up" list screen is gone: it was a list of the same people, with the same balances, that you tapped in order to reach a list — and the hero's Settle button was removed for that exact reason a release earlier. The picker row at the top is for changing your mind, not for arriving.

Everything arrives pre-answered. Person = whoever you came from. Amount = the full balance. Method = UPI when they have a UPI ID. The common case is open, read, tap.

Partial payment is an edit to the amount, not a mode. The field holds the full balance and you can change it. "Half" was a third button for a figure nobody owes, sitting beside the one figure that is always right.

Three things the built screen grew that are not here, and go. A row of UPI app chips — GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM — where any already opens the system chooser, which lists the apps that are actually installed; picking a named one you do not have just fails. A "Where this comes from" group breakdown, which is a read, not a decision, and is on the person's ledger one tap away. And a currency strip that renders for everyone although almost nobody has two — one line, and only when there is genuinely a second.

Aakalan never sees a UPI PIN. The button hands off to the user's UPI app by intent. There is no card form anywhere in this product and there never will be.

Frame 2 is the they owe me direction. There is nothing to pay, so the two honest actions are to record what arrived — which is now simply true when you say it — or to ask.
