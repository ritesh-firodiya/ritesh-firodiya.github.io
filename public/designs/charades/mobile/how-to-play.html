How to play

1

Pick your packs

Choose which decks are in the game. Mix Hollywood, Family & Kids, or Party.

2

Hold the phone up

Place it against your forehead, screen facing out, so friends can see the card but you can't.

3

Act it out

Tilt down when you guess it. Tilt up to skip.

4

Score before time runs out

Rack up as many correct guesses as you can. Post-round: you'll see everything you scored on and skipped.

Tip · Stuck on a movie?

Tap the Hint chip to reveal the year. Tap again to reveal year + cast.
