About

I focus on salaried Indians with at least one of the following: equity comp (RSU/ESOP), capital gains, foreign assets, or a §143/§148 notice. I add value on the messier returns.

For the ITR Filing service: I'll review the Tax Review you generated, fix anything the engine wasn't sure about (HRA proof gaps, capital-gains lot matching, foreign-asset valuation date conventions), prepare your return, and file it on incometax.gov.in from my CA console. ITR-V acknowledgement back to your Inbox.

For Notice & Defence: the ITR Filing tier plus 12 months of §143/§148 notice response support. If a notice arrives, I draft the response, attach evidence from your Inbox, and submit on the portal. No extra charge per notice in those 12 months.
