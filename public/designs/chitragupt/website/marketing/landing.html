FAQ

Common questions from salaried filers.

Do you file my ITR for me?

Not directly today. We're applying for the ERI licence; once we hold it, you'll file directly through Chitragupt. Until then, we hand you a CA-ready packet and connect you to a verified CA.

What if the engine is wrong?

Every number cites the document it came from, and every rule references the relevant section of the Income Tax Act. If edge cases (Schedule FA, mid-year residency change, business income) apply, you'll see a clear note about it — we don't bluff.

Is the AI advisor live?

Yes. AI Self and Pro Family both include it — 5 questions a day on your own documents, with every answer linked back to the source document. Quota resets at midnight IST. Pro Family shares the daily quota across the whole household.

Can I share my Tax Review with my own CA?

Yes. Settings → Sharing → Invite a CA. They get an invite link to read your confirmed documents and Tax Review (read-only, scoped to the AYs you pick, revocable any time). Free.

What happens to my data if I delete my account?

Settings → Privacy → Delete account. 7 days grace window (cancellable). After that, every document, ledger entry, and analyze output is wiped. You receive a deletion certificate via email.

Why does Form 16 alone get to a number?

For most salaried filers, Form 16 carries gross salary, employee PF, professional tax, 80C, 80D, HRA exemption, and TDS — everything the engine needs. We surface "where 26AS or AIS would add more" right next to the headline number so you know what's missing.

Do you store my Aadhaar number?

We don't extract Aadhaar fields from your documents into our database. If you upload a document image (your Aadhaar card itself, for example) into the Inbox for safekeeping, it stays in your private vault — only you and people you explicitly share with can see it.
