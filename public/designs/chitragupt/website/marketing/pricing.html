FAQ · pricing

Is ₹249 a trial?
No — it's a one-time unlock for AY 2026-27. Refundable for 14 days from unlock.
What's the difference between Self and AI Self?
Self (₹249) gives you the full Tax / Expense / Portfolio engine. AI Self (₹499) adds 5 AI questions per day on your own parsed documents — every answer cites the source doc. Already on Self? Top up ₹250 any time.
Is this a subscription?
No. You pay once per AY. AY 2027-28 next year is a separate unlock (₹249 Self · ₹499 AI Self · ₹799 Pro Family).
How do upgrades work?
Self → AI Self: top up ₹250. Self → Pro Family: top up ₹550. AI Self → Pro Family: top up ₹300. Each top-up gets its own fresh 14-day refund window.
What's the refund policy?
Refundable for 14 days from unlock. Refunds apply separately to each payment (unlock, AI Self top-up, Pro Family top-up), each with its own 14-day window.
How many PANs does Pro Family cover?
Up to 6 PANs in one household — you + spouse + parents + children. One ₹799 unlock per AY covers the whole bundle.
