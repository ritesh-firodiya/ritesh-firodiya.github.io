Refund Policy

Version 1.0 · effective 23 May 2026

Headline

Refundable for 14 days from unlock. One-click from Settings → Billing.

Per-payment principle · load-bearing

Every payment has its own 14-day refund window.

The original Self (₹249), the Pro Family upgrade top-up (₹550), the full Pro Family (₹799), and every hire-a-CA payment all carry independent 14-day refund windows from the moment of payment. The clock on one payment does not consume the clock on the next.

Hire-a-CA refund rules (full / 50% / none based on engagement state) are unchanged — but each payment inside an engagement keeps its own clock.

Self · ₹249

Tax Review unlock

  • Full refund · 14 days from unlock
  • Razorpay credits back to source in 5-7 working days

Pro Family · ₹799

Household bundle · up to 6 PANs

  • Full refund · 14 days from unlock
  • Self → Pro Family top-up (₹550) refunds standalone · 14 days from the top-up itself
  • Refunding the top-up reverts you to Self · the original ₹249 keeps its own (already-elapsed) window
  • Family members keep their Paid unlocks if Pro Family is refunded

Hire a CA · marketplace

Consultation / ITR Filing / Notice & Defence (CAs set their own prices)

  • Full refund · CA doesn't accept within the matching window
  • Full refund · CA accepts then cancels before any deliverable
  • 50% refund · CA accepted, you cancel before any deliverable · within 14 days of that payment
  • No refund · once the CA has delivered (filed ITR, written advice, notice reply)
  • Tier upgrade top-ups · independent 14-day window from the top-up's capture (see row below)

Hire a CA · tier upgrade mid-engagement

Example · Consultation → ITR Filing mid-chat

  • The top-up payment has its own 14-day refund window from the moment it captures.
  • The original Consultation fee is consumed and non-refundable — the engagement has progressed past initial acceptance.
  • Refunding the top-up reverts the engagement to Consultation · the CA is notified and the revenue share is recomputed on the lower total.
  • The top-up forfeits its window once an ITR Filing deliverable (filed ITR / ITR-V) or a Notice & Defence response is dispatched — whichever applies to the upgraded tier.

FAQ · refunds

How do I request a refund?
From Settings → Billing · click "Request a refund" next to the relevant charge. Approval is automatic if you're inside the 14-day window.
What if your engine got my tax wrong?
Email support with the issue. If we agree it's a computation error, we'll refund regardless of the 14-day window or export status — and fix it for everyone.
How long does the refund take?
Razorpay sends a credit back to your original payment method · UPI usually settles same-day, cards 5-7 working days, netbanking 3-5. We don't hold the money on our end.
Can I cancel Pro Family mid-year?
Pro Family is one-time per AY, not a subscription — there's nothing to cancel. If you upgraded Self → Pro Family and want to step back inside the top-up's 14-day window, we refund the ₹550 top-up standalone and downgrade you to Self.
Does refunding a hire-a-CA top-up reset the original Consultation fee's clock?
No. Each payment has its own clock from the moment it captured. Refunding the upgrade top-up reverts the engagement to the lower tier · the original Consultation fee's window is independent (and, in nearly every case, already elapsed and consumed because the engagement progressed).
