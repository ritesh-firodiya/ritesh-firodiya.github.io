1 · What Chitragupt is

A software tool that organises your tax documents and computes your tax position under both the Old and New regimes. We surface unclaimed deductions and produce a Tax Review your Chartered Accountant can use to file your return. Chitragupt does not file returns. We are not a chartered accountant, not a tax preparer, and not a representative of the Income Tax Department.
