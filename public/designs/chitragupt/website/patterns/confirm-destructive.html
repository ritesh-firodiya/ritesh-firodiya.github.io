Pattern · destructive confirm

UI pattern sheet

Destructive confirm modal · one pattern, three intensities

Use the same modal shape everywhere a user is about to do something destructive. The intensity (amber → rose → "type to confirm") scales with how hard the action is to undo.

Anatomy

  • Title · names the thing being destroyed, in the user's words
  • What stays · what goes · two columns, never a wall of text
  • Reversibility line · "you can restore for 7 days" or "this cannot be undone"
  • Friction · type-to-confirm only at level 3
  • Cancel is on the left, destructive on the right · always
  • Esc closes · click outside closes · enter does not confirm

When to use which

L1 · softDelete document · remove approved sender · cancel pending invite
L2 · hardLeave household · cancel subscription · revoke CA share
L3 · permanentDissolve household · delete account · purge all data
