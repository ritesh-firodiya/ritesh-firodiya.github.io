Re-check your word

The one screen no competitor ships. Straight out of Imposter Who?'s reviews: "a button that lets people who have forgotten what they are re-check it would be very helpful — me and my friend are always forgetting what word we're on." v5 deleted thirteen screens and kept this one, because it is the only one that answers a complaint nobody else has answered.

Reached from the ? in the talk screen's header, and gated the same way the first card is: pick your own name, then hold to show.

Hold, not tap. A tap can be triggered by someone grabbing the phone; a press-and-hold that hides on release cannot be left face-up on the table. It is the cheapest possible protection against the one way this feature could break the game.

Peek counts live here and nowhere else now. They used to surface on the round result too; with no scoring left there is nothing to weigh them against, and a tally on the answer screen would read as an accusation the app is not entitled to make.

Avatars are lucide, not emoji — the same correction as everywhere else. This list exists to tell five people apart, which is precisely where a row of tofu boxes does the most damage.
