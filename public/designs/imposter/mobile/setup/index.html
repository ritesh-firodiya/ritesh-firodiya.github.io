Setup is the app

v5 — the home screen is gone. Launch lands here. There is no hero screen with a PLAY button in front of a setup wizard, because there was never a reason for one: a party game is opened with five people already waiting, and the first thing anyone needs is the thing they are about to change.

Checked against the market, not guessed. Imposter Who? (5M+ installs) launches straight onto four drill-down rows. imposter.app is one list and a Start button. Neither ships a home screen, and neither ships a three-step setup wizard.

v4 had four ways to start a game — NEW GAME, "Same 5 again", a category tile, and "All 10 →" — each landing in a different step of players → roles → category. Tapping a category set the pack and then made you pick it again two screens later. One screen cannot contradict itself.

Two rows drill down, three do not. Players and Packs open a sheet because their content is unbounded — 20 names, 10 packs. Imposters, hints and the clock are one control each and stay here. A sheet for a stepper is a screen that exists to hold three taps.

The screen height never changes. Three players or twenty, one pack or ten, it is the same five rows. That is the whole reason the unbounded things sit behind a chevron.

The CTA states what is missing rather than greying out with no reason — "Add 1 more player", not a dead button. Rule 9.

Hints stay a row rather than becoming a buried preference. A hand-written hint for every word is the one thing no competitor advertises, because no competitor writes them.
