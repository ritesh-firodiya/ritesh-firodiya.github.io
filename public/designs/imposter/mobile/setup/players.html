Names are remembered between games. The second round is the one that actually gets played, and re-typing five names at a table is exactly where a group gives up and puts the phone down.

The field stays focused and the keyboard stays up — type, Enter, type, Enter. Tapping a name edits it in place; the × removes.

Minimum three. With two there is nobody to suspect. The CTA states the number missing rather than sitting greyed out with no reason — a disabled control with no explanation is a dead end.

Avatars are lucide icons on a neutral plate, never emoji. The first build shipped emoji here and every one rendered as a tofu box on the simulator: five identical grey discs, in the one place the game has to tell people apart. Emoji are a font lookup and degrade silently; lucide draws through SVG and cannot.

Removing a player re-seats the wheel. Two players three icons apart after a deletion is a visible accident.
