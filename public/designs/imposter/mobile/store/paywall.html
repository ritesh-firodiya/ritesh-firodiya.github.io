The paywall

Redrawn. A white page with a $4.99 and three ticks looked apologetic. It is the same offer now, stated with the confidence the competitors state theirs with — full-bleed stage, ten tiles you can actually see, one enormous price.

One price, once, and the promise above it. Every dark pattern this category runs on is deliberately absent: no countdown, no strikethrough "was $19.99", no "3 days free then billed weekly", no pre-selected annual plan, no delayed close button.

That absence is the product. Fakeit's reviews say "$15.99 a week is insanity"; Imposter Up's say a lifetime purchase re-locked itself. Any urgency device under our sentence would make the sentence a lie.

Restore is in the header, above the fold. App Store review requires it for a non-consumable, and it is the first thing a locked-out payer hunts for.
