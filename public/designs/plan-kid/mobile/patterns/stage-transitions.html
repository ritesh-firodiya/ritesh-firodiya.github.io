Stage Transitions

Purpose: Pattern reference for emotional moment transitions between app stages.

Key features:

  • Trying to pregnancy celebration
  • Pregnancy to postpartum transition
  • Loss and sensitive transitions

Why: Transitions are moments — not modal walls. Trying → pregnancy is celebration + informed handoff. Pregnancy → postpartum is warmer than clinical. Loss milestones get their own flow with no celebration language.
