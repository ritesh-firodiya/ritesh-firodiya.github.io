Toasts & Banners

Purpose: Pattern reference for toast notifications and inline banners.

Key features:

  • Success toasts with minimal copy
  • Info notifications with CTAs
  • Warning banners
  • Red-flag alert banners

Why: Success is one word. Info is a sentence. Warning is soft. Red-flag banners are always inline (never modal). No "toast celebrations" for medical events.
