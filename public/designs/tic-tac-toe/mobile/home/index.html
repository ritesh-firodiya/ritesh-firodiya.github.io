Four cards, one tap to play. Mode and difficulty are the same choice, not two screens. "Two players" is one card; the computer is three. A second screen to pick difficulty would put a decision between the player and a two-minute game.

The difficulty ramp is vertical. Easy / Medium / Hard sit under one another and are read by comparison, so they get one green→amber→rose ramp rather than three unrelated hues.

Hard is honest. It is perfect minimax — it cannot be beaten, only drawn. The label says so, because a player who loses ten times to an unlabelled "Hard" concludes the app cheats.

Both themes are real. app.config.ts sets userInterfaceStyle: "automatic", so neither is the default. Drawn side by side for that reason.

No scores, no streaks, no account. The only persisted state is the last mode and difficulty, in MMKV. Anything else would need a backend this product does not have.
