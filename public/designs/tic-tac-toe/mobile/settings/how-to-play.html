Everyone knows the rules. This screen exists for the two things they do not know: what the three difficulties actually do, and that Hard cannot be beaten. It is not a tutorial.

Hard is stated plainly. "You can draw, not win" is the single most useful sentence here. Without it, a player who loses repeatedly concludes the app cheats — and says so in a review.

A worked diagram, not prose. One small board showing a completed line does more than a paragraph describing one.

Reachable from everywhere. The circle-help button sits in the header on both Home and Board, so the answer is never more than one tap away.

The three difficulties

Easy

Moves at random. Good for a five-year-old.

Medium

Plays well most of the time, and slips often enough that you can win.

Hard

Perfect play. It cannot be beaten — the best you can do is force a draw.
