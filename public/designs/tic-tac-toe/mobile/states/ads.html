Ad states — src/lib/ads.ts

This is the app's only revenue. No IAP, no subscription, no RevenueCat. AdMob banner plus interstitial, and that is the whole model — which is why the states it can be in are part of the spec rather than an implementation detail.

The banner reserves its height whether or not it fills. A banner that appears late pushes the board down mid-game and costs a misplaced tap. The slot is 320×50 and it is always there.

Ads off is a real state. EXPO_PUBLIC_SHOW_ADS gates the banner, and ads.web.ts is a no-op shim so the web export carries no ad code at all. Both have to look right.

Never between the winning move and the winning line. An interstitial belongs after the result sheet is dismissed. Showing one before the player sees how they won is the single most punished thing in this category's reviews.

The listings must say ads are present. app-summary.mdx once claimed "No Ads" while the production profile shipped both formats.
