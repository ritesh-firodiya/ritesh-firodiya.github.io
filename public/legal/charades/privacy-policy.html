Privacy Policy

Charades - Act it Out! — The Charades Party Game
Last updated: September 2026

Charades - Act it Out! does not collect your personal data. The app has no ads, no analytics and no user accounts. Your settings, packs and game history stay on your device. We do not run servers that store your personal data.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK, and it does not send your usage, your device identifiers or your game data to us.

The app can be played entirely offline. Network access is used only to load pack prices and to complete a purchase you start yourself.

Data Stored On Your Device

The following is stored locally and never transmitted to us:

  • Settings (haptics, vibration, sounds, tilt, language)
  • Selected and unlocked packs
  • Game history (capped at the most recent 50 rounds)
  • Whether you have completed onboarding

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

Purchases

The app sells one-time pack unlocks. There is no subscription and no advertising.

Google Play processes the payment. We never see or handle your card or billing details.

RevenueCat — purchase and entitlement state is managed by RevenueCat so your unlocked packs can be restored on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and the purchase receipt from the store. It does not receive your name, email or payment details from us. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or rent personal information. There is no advertising network and no analytics provider in this app, so there is nothing to share with one.

No Account Required

The app does not require sign-up, login or any account creation. It works immediately after installation.

Children's Privacy

The app includes a Family & Kids category of kid-safe cards and is suitable for mixed-age play. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the app under Settings > Privacy Policy.
