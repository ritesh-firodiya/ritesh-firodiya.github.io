Privacy Policy

Chess — Classic Game
Last updated: October 2026

Chess keeps your games on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. The only thing that leaves your device is the request for an advertisement.

What the app stores

These stay on your device and are never sent to us:

  • The game mode you last chose (two players, or against the computer)
  • The difficulty you last chose

A game in progress lives in memory while you play. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address or phone number — the app never asks for one
  • No analytics or usage tracking of our own
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone

Advertising

Chess is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner, and a full-screen ad between games. The app asks AdMob for non-personalised ads only.

To deliver and measure an ad, AdMob receives information from your device such as its IP address, device and operating-system details, and an advertising identifier. That information goes to Google, not to us. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings: on Android under Settings → Google → Ads, on iPhone under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell your information and we have none to sell. Nothing is shared with anyone other than what the ad request above sends to Google.

No account, nothing to delete

Because there is no account and no data on a server of ours, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

Chess is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app requests non-personalised ads only.
