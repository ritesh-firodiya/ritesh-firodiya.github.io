Delete your Chitragupt account

Chitragupt (com.chitragupt.app), published by Ritesh Firodiya
Last updated: September 2026

You can delete your Chitragupt account and everything in it yourself, at any time, without asking us and without giving a reason. The Android app and chitragupt.ai are the same account — deleting it in one place deletes it in both.

In the app

  1. Open Chitragupt and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Continue to deletion.
  4. Tap Schedule deletion to confirm.

On the website the same thing lives at Settings → Privacy + Data → Delete account, where you type DELETE to confirm.

You get 7 days to change your mind

After you confirm, a 7-day countdown starts. Sign in during those 7 days and tap Cancel deletion to stop it.

Once the 7 days are up, the wipe runs and nothing can be recovered — including by us.

What we must keep, and why

Indian tax law requires us to keep records of payments for 7 years. Those records stay, but your name, PAN and contact details are stripped out of them, so they are no longer about an identifiable person.

DataWhat happens
Payment recordsKept for 7 years, with your name removed. Required by Income-Tax record-keeping rules.
Support messagesKept, with your name removed.
Counters like "how many reviews ran this month"Kept. They hold no personal detail.
Your deletion certificateEmailed to you once the wipe has run, so you have proof it happened.

Delete only some of your data

You do not have to close your account to remove things.

  • One document — open it in Inbox and tap Delete. The file and every number we read out of it go at once. There is no bin to restore it from.
  • What a CA can see — Settings → Sharing & CA links → revoke. Their access ends immediately.
  • Your household — Settings → Family → leave. Your papers stop being visible to the household owner.
  • Anything else — email support@chitragupt.ai and say what you want removed. We do it within 7 days and confirm by email.

Take a copy first

Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data emails you a ZIP of your documents, your profile and your ledger. Do this before you delete — we cannot get it back for you afterwards.

Can't sign in?

Email support@chitragupt.ai from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We check that it is you, then run the same wipe.
