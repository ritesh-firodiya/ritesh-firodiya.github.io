Delete your Imposter data

Imposter Game: Find the Liar (com.ritvi.imposter), published by Ritvi Apps — Ritesh Firodiya
Last updated: 12 October 2026

Imposter has no accounts. There is no email, phone number or password to delete. This page says what the app keeps, what deletes itself, and how to ask us to delete the rest.

What deletes itself

  1. Online rooms. A room, the names and icons in it, the cards, the one-word clues, the votes and the scores are deleted automatically. A room expires a day after the last thing that happened in it, and deletion normally completes within two days of that.
  2. Everything on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes your tables, scores, settings, your own words and the app's random ID.

How to ask us to delete data

  1. Email firodiya.ritesh@gmail.com with “Delete my Imposter data” in the subject.
  2. Tell us the four-letter room code and roughly when you played. We do not know who you are, so the room is the only way we can find your data.
  3. We reply to confirm, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can ask for any reason, without giving one.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
The room: names, icons, cards, clues, votes, scoresDeleted, at once on request, or by itself within about two days.
The count of rooms your random ID has madeDeleted on request.
The random ID itselfDeleted on request. It names nobody: there is no email or phone number behind it.

What is kept, and why

Purchase records stay with the store. Apple or Google took the payment and keep their own record of it, as the law requires of them. RevenueCat keeps the receipt against an anonymous ID so a purchase can be restored. Ask in the same email and we will have the RevenueCat record removed too.
