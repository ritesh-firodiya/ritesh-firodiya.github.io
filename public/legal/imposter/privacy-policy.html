Privacy Policy

Imposter: Find the Liar — the social deduction party game
Last updated: September 2026

Imposter: Find the Liar does not collect your personal data. The app has no ads, no analytics and no user accounts. Your players, packs and settings stay on your device. We run no servers that store your personal data.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK, and it does not send your usage, your device identifiers or your game data to us.

The game is played entirely offline on one device. Network access is used only to look up the unlock price and to complete a purchase you start yourself.

Data Stored On Your Device

The following is stored locally and never transmitted to us:

  • The player names you type, and the icon assigned to each
  • Your round settings (word packs, number of imposters, hints, talking time)
  • Whether haptics are on
  • Whether you have completed onboarding

Player names are typed by you and are used only to label the cards during a round. A round in progress is never written to disk at all. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything above.

Purchases

The app sells a single one-time unlock for the remaining word packs. There is no subscription and no advertising.

The App Store and Google Play process the payment. We never see or handle your card or billing details.

RevenueCat — purchase and entitlement state is managed by RevenueCat so your unlock can be restored on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and the purchase receipt from the store. It does not receive your name, email or payment details from us. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or rent personal information. There is no advertising network and no analytics provider in this app, so there is nothing to share with one.

No Account Required

The app does not require sign-up, login or any account creation. It works immediately after installation.

Children's Privacy

The app is a word game played out loud in a group. One optional word pack, "After Dark", contains adult themes and is not included in the free packs. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the app under Settings > Privacy Policy.
