Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe — Classic Game with AI Opponent
Last updated: April 2026

Tic Tac Toe respects your privacy. All game data and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not run servers that store your personal data.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our service:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (for analytics only)
  • Local Settings: Game preferences (game mode, difficulty, statistics) stored locally on your device only
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous usage data to understand how the app is used and improve performance

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Display ads through Google AdMob
  • Analyze app usage and improve user experience
  • Ensure app security and prevent abuse

Local Data

The following are stored on your device only and never transmitted to a server:

  • Game settings (haptics, sound, etc.)
  • Selected game mode and difficulty
  • Game history and statistics

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad-interaction data to provide personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your personal information. Aggregated, anonymized data may be shared with Google AdMob and analytics providers to improve the app.

No Account Required

Tic Tac Toe does not require sign-up, login, or any account creation. The app works immediately after installation.

Children's Privacy

Tic Tac Toe is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

  • Access your locally stored data
  • Delete your data by uninstalling the app
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available in the app via the Privacy Policy link on the home screen.
