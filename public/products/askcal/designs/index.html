The one idea

Every competitor turns a photo into one confident number. A photo cannot show cooking fat, the inside of a mixed dish, or whose share of a shared plate this is — so that number is often wrong, and worst on exactly the food most of the world eats. AskCal estimates, then asks at most two questions the camera cannot answer, and resolves the answer against USDA data rather than the model's memory. Amber means the app is asking. It is used for nothing else.

Conventions

  • Tokens live in tailwind.config.js; components in shared.css. Mirrored, so either works.
  • Colours are named by role, never hue — brand, ask, conf.
  • Amber = the app is asking. Never decorative.
  • Every estimate carries a confidence: Seen Guessed Unknown
  • All figures use .num for tabular alignment. Integers only — no fractional grams.
  • Every screen lives in exactly one folder, and the breadcrumb lists that folder in full. Same chips, same order, on every screen in the group — so the strip is a map, not a guess. Cross-group jumps are the tab bar's job, not the breadcrumb's.
  • Status lives on the gallery card, never in the section heading. A screen must not move sections the day it is approved — that is how the same screen ends up listed twice.
  • Numbers in the wireframes are one persona, computed, not invented — male, 34, 178 cm, 80 kg, 1–3 workouts a week. That is BMR 1,748 → TDEE 2,272 → maintain 2,272, protein 144 g. If a screen shows a figure, it must be derivable from those.
