Why this app

Every incumbent (Flo, What to Expect, BabyCenter, Ovia, iMumz, MyloFamily, BabyChakra) is mom-only. India community/UGC plays (Momspresso, TinyStep) have all died. The AI wave just cracked the vertical open (Soula Care, Aware.ai). Pattaya's wedge: couples-first + AI coach + India-native cultural depth. Subscription-only (₹399/mo · ₹2,499/yr household). No community. No marketplace. No ads. See .context/documents/wiki/decisions/ for the three founding ADRs.
